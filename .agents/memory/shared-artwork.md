---
name: Shared department artwork
description: Why R&D and Operations reuse Marketing artwork rather than legacy R&D artwork.
---

Keep the new R&D and Operations Artwork tabs on the same records as Marketing. Do not automatically combine or migrate the legacy R&D artwork dataset.

**Why:** The existing R&D artwork uses a different schema from the Marketing tracker. Reusing the current tracker avoids duplicate collaboration records; migrating legacy content would require a separate field-mapping decision, not just adding navigation tabs.

**How to apply:** Extend the shared tracker for cross-department artwork changes. Preserve legacy records unless the user authorizes a migration.
